About Taqtaqa

Taqtaqa (طقطقة) is a free Arabic typing test and Arabic keyboard trainer, like Monkeytype but made for Arabic. Instead of random words, you type real Egyptian and Saudi proverbs, famous lines from Egyptian films, Juha folk tales, wisdom and classical Arabic poetry. There is also a tab of English quotes.

How you practice

  • Timed tests of 15, 30, 60 or 120 seconds, with words per minute and accuracy.
  • Word count and full quote modes to finish a text from start to end.
  • Arabic diacritics (tashkeel): turn on diacritics in Settings and every Arabic text shows simple marks (shadda, fatha, kasra, damma and tanween) that you type along with the letters.
  • Practice your mistakes: after each test, practice the exact words you got wrong.

What helps you

  • On-screen Arabic keyboard for the Windows (Arabic 101) and Mac layouts that lights up the next key, including when you need Shift.
  • History and personal bests are saved, and if you sign in with Google or email they follow you across devices.
  • Works on desktop and mobile. You can install it as an app, and it keeps working offline. No ads and no paywall.
