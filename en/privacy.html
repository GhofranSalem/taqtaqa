Privacy at Taqtaqa

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Without an account

If you use the site without signing in, your test results and settings are saved in your browser only. Nobody else can see them, and they are deleted if you clear your browser data.

If you create an account

We save in your account:

  • Your email and name, and your photo if you sign in with Google.
  • Your test results: speed, accuracy, time and test type, plus your personal bests.
  • The texts you saved to Favorites, and your speed last time on each text you typed.

This data is stored on Google's Firebase, and only you can read it. We don't sell it, share it with anyone, or use it for ads.

Visit counts

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see how many people visit the site, which pages they open, and how fast they load. It sets no cookies, doesn't track you across sites, and doesn't know who you are.

If you send us a message

Messages you send from Support Taqtaqa reach us by email, along with the email you entered if you entered one, and the text that was on your screen at the time. We only use them to reply to you and fix the site.

Deleting your data

From your account button at the top, click Delete my account and all my data, and the account and all its results are deleted for good. You can also clear just your history from the History page.

Questions?

Email us at hello@taqtaqa.com, or use the Support Taqtaqa button at the bottom of the home page.
